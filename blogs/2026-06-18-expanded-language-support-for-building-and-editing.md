Gemini in Sheets adds support for 28 additional languages, including Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean, letting users collaborate natively with Gemini in their preferred language. The feature uses Sheets tools like tables, pivot tables, charts, and formulas to execute user prompts.
