Google Vids expands default avatars from 23 to 53 presets spanning photorealistic, 3D cartoon, and graphic novel styles, and grows language support to 24 including Hindi, Bengali, and Arabic. Custom avatars now support 30+ Gemini Audio voices and can be directed to take action in any generated video beyond speaking.
